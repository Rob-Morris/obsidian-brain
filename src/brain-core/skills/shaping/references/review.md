# Optional four-Cs review

Recommend this review when an active workflow reaches a candidate handoff or
completion point. It is a quality option, not a required gate. Honour an
explicit earlier request for the review as a mid-pass check without implying
that the artefact is ready to finish.

Explain the current completion bar, why the artefact appears to meet it, and
that the review checks correctness, clarity, consistency, and completeness.
Then ask whether the user would like the review, to proceed without it, or to
stop the current pass. Keep the recommendation proportional to the artefact and
available review capability.

## Review context

Read the current artefact, its completion bar, and directly related material
needed to check its claims or consistency. Consult a transcript only when
provenance is necessary. Use a separate read-only reviewer when one is available
and proportionate; otherwise make a clearly separated pass.

Apply the current workflow’s meaning of completeness:

- **Brainstorm:** purpose, boundaries, and the decision/work surface are clear
  enough to hand to refine.
- **Refine:** the agreed bar is met and no material decision or required agent
  work remains unresolved.
- **Discover:** the bar is met for the user’s intended scope, without demanding
  exhaustive territory.

## Four Cs

1. **Correctness:** Claims, constraints, examples, links, and accepted outcomes
   are accurate and supported.
2. **Clarity:** A fresh reader can understand the purpose, boundaries,
   terminology, and intended use without the shaping conversation.
3. **Consistency:** Sections, decisions, related material, and any lifecycle
   state agree.
4. **Completeness:** The current workflow’s completion bar is met, with no
   material placeholder, unresolved ambiguity, or unfinished required work.

## Handle the result

Return evidence-backed findings, ranked as blocking, material, or minor. The
main shaping agent validates and deduplicates them, then presents a compact
inventory and asks for one commitment at a time.

Accepted findings re-enter the active workflow and are recorded using the
session plan. Declined findings stay out of the active agenda unless changed
evidence or the user’s later direction makes them relevant. After accepted fixes,
assess the candidate exit again against the same completion bar.
