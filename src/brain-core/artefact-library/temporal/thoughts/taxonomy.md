# Thoughts

Temporal artefact. Raw, unformed thinking captured in the moment.

## Purpose

A thought is the precursor to an idea — rougher, more fleeting, possibly just a sentence or a question. Thoughts are temporal because they record what surfaced at a particular moment. The bar for capture is deliberately low: if something crosses your mind and feels worth noting, write it down. Most thoughts won't go anywhere, and that's fine. Some will spark ideas, writing, designs, or other artefacts.

## Graduation

A thought can become anything it makes sense to become. There is no prescribed path. When a thought does inspire a new artefact, follow [[.brain-core/standards/provenance]].

## Naming

`yyyymmdd-thought~{Title}.md` in `_Temporal/Thoughts/`.

Example: `_Temporal/Thoughts/20260320-thought~Writing as Thinking.md`

## Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `shaping` | The artefact is being shaped through structured Q&A. |
| `ready` | Shaping is complete and the artefact meets its bar. |

## Shaping

**Flavour:** Discovery
**Bar:** The thought is captured faithfully enough to preserve what surfaced; uncertainty or incompleteness may remain when represented honestly.
**Completion status:** `ready`

See [[.brain-core/standards/shaping]] for the shaping process.

## Trigger

When a raw thought surfaces during a session, capture it before it slips away.
